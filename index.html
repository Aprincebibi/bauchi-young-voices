About Bauchi Young Voices

We are a youth-led organization dedicated to creating platforms for young people in Bauchi State to express themselves, develop skills, and contribute to community development.

Our Mission & Vision

Bauchi State Young Voices is committed to empowering youth through advocacy, education, and leadership development. We believe in the transformative power of young people when given the right platforms and opportunities.

Founded in 2020, we have engaged over 5,000 young people across Bauchi State in various programs ranging from civic education to entrepreneurship training.

Our vision is to create a Bauchi State where every young person has a voice, access to opportunities, and the capacity to contribute meaningfully to society.

Our Programs

We offer diverse programs designed to equip young people with skills, knowledge, and platforms for expression.

Youth Advocacy

Training young people in advocacy skills to effectively engage with policymakers and community leaders on issues affecting youth.

Digital Skills

Providing training in digital literacy, coding, and online content creation to prepare youth for the digital economy.

Entrepreneurship

Supporting young entrepreneurs with training, mentorship, and access to startup resources to launch sustainable businesses.
